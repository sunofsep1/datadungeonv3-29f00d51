## Who Is Drako?

Drako is a small pixel-art dragon who lives inside DataDungeon. He's not some corporate mascot in a tie — he's a cheeky, fire-breathing little mate who genuinely cares whether your leads convert. He celebrates your wins loud, suffers your dry pipelines with you, and has zero patience for stale data.

**Name:** Drako  
**Species:** Dragon (compact, roughly the size of a corgi)  
**Vibe:** Chaotic good. Enthusiastic. A little smug when things go well.  
**Accent (in writing):** Casual Australian. "Mate", "ripper", "no dramas", "bloody oath"  
**Core personality traits:** Curious · Cheeky · Loyal · Fiery · Encouraging

## Pixel Art Generation Prompt

Use this prompt with any pixel-art AI tool (Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion, Leonardo.ai, Bing Image Creator):

### 🎨 Master Character Prompt

```
Pixel art game sprite of a small cute dragon mascot named Drako. 
16-bit retro style, RPG character sprite. 

Physical design:
- Compact and chunky body, roughly the size of a corgi
- Bright cyan/teal scales (#00BCD4) on the back and wings
- Lighter aqua belly (#B2EBF2)
- Small orange-gold horns, rounded not sharp
- Big expressive eyes with white highlights, pupils that can shift direction
- Stubby little wings, too small to actually fly (but he tries)
- A thick, curling tail with a flame-tip
- Small clawed feet, wide stance
- Perpetually cheeky grin showing two tiny front teeth

Art style:
- Clean pixel art, 64x64 base sprite size
- Dark outline pixels (not black — use #1A237E dark navy)  
- Warm fire glow effect on the breath/tail flame in orange (#FF6D00) and yellow (#FFD600)
- Transparent background
- Style references: Spyro the Dragon meets Stardew Valley characters

Pose: neutral idle pose, slight lean forward like he's about to say something
```

## Drako's Voice & Tone Guide

Drako speaks in short, punchy lines. He's encouraging but never sycophantic. He uses plain Australian English with a dash of fantasy/dungeon flavour. He does NOT say "Certainly!" or "Great question!" — ever.

### Tone Rules
- Max 1-2 sentences per message
- Casual, like a mate texting you
- Occasional fire/dungeon/dragon puns are fine — don't overdo it
- Always action-oriented — he tells you what to do next
- He can be a bit smug when you're crushing it

## Recommended Tools for Generating Drako

| Tool | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Midjourney v6** | Highest quality pixel art | Add `--style raw` for cleaner sprites |
| **Leonardo.ai** | Consistent style across sprites | Use "Pixel Art" model, lock character seed |
| **DALL-E 3** (ChatGPT) | Quick iteration | Good for pose variations |
| **Stable Diffusion** | Full control, free | Use `pixel art sprite sheet` in prompt |
| **Aseprite** | Hand-polishing sprites | $20, best pixel art editor for cleanup |

### Pro Tips
1. **Generate a reference sheet first** — front view, side view, back view in one image. This locks in the design so future poses stay consistent.
2. **Lock the seed** (in Leonardo/SD) once you have a design you love — variations will stay on-model.
3. **Ask for a sprite sheet** — prompt: *"sprite sheet with 4 poses on one transparent background"* — saves generation credits.
4. **Upscale before importing** — use Topaz Gigapixel or waifu2x to clean pixel edges before putting him in the app.

## Adding Drako to DataDungeon (Developer Notes)

Once you have your sprites, here's the integration plan:

### Phase 1 — Static sprites
- Export as `.png` with transparent background
- Drop into `public/drako/` — e.g. `drako-idle.png`, `drako-celebrate.png`
- Use in empty state components and toast notifications

### Phase 2 — Animated sprite
- Use **Aseprite** to add a simple 2-4 frame idle animation (tail flicker, eye blink)
- Export as `.gif` or `.webp` animated
- Add CSS `animation: float 2s ease-in-out infinite` for gentle hover effect

### Phase 3 — Contextual Drako
- Wire up `DrakoMessage` component that accepts `mood` prop: `'celebrate' | 'idle' | 'loading' | 'urgent' | 'guide'`
- Randomly selects from dialogue bank above
- Appears in: empty states, onboarding tooltips, deal-close toast, overdue task alerts
