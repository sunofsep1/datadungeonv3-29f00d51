# Greg Leigh — Growth Roadmap (July → November 2026)
*One-man band. Tight budget. Spring is the harvest — August is the planting. Written 25 July 2026.*

## The one-sentence strategy
Stop buying strangers and start harvesting the 709 people who already know you — the ads' job is only to top up the funnel, the platform's job is to do your admin, and **your** job is 2 hours of real conversations a day.

## The maths that matters (why database-first)
- A cold Meta lead costs $15–40 and converts to a listing at maybe 1–3%.
- A past client, past appraisal enquirer, or open-home buyer costs **$0** and lists with the agent who stayed in touch. Industry-wide, most agents lose ~80% of repeat/referral business simply by going quiet.
- You have 709 contacts, 601 properties, 23 listings-tracked, and a CRM that scores, sequences and reminds automatically. That asset out-earns any ad budget — if it gets worked.

## PHASE 0 — This week (all $0, all leverage)
1. **Turn the taps on**: Pocket → notes@, Outlook → leads@ (5 min). Send Insider #1 to the 196. Done = your whole machine is finally live.
2. **The Big Sort (one session, ~90 min with me)**: we run through the database together and bucket every contact: A = knows me, could sell within 12 months · B = knows me, long-term · C = cold/dead. I'll pre-rank them using lead scores, last-touch dates and property data so you're just confirming. Output: your A-list (probably 40–80 people). That list IS your spring campaign.
3. **Daily 5**: the CRM already ranks urgency. New habit — five A-list calls a day, logged by voice note through Pocket (zero typing). 25 conversations/week ≈ 100 by spring. That's how one-man bands out-list franchises.

## PHASE 1 — August: plant the spring pipeline
**Goal: 3 appraisals booked per week (12+ in August). Every August appraisal is a September listing candidate.**
- **The valuation-letter play ($0):** every Redlands owner just got a +20% land valuation letter. It's the best conversation-starter in years. Script: "Did yours jump too? Want the number the letter can't tell you?" Use on calls, SMS, and the newsletter (already the Insider lead story).
- **The splittable-block play ($0, nobody else is doing it):** City Plan changes make some 600sqm+/corner blocks subdividable. We can query YOUR 601 properties for candidates and you call those owners with genuinely valuable news. That's not prospecting, that's a favour.
- **Buyer-to-seller flip ($0):** every buyer enquiry the bridge captures gets asked one question in your follow-up: "Do you have a home to sell first?" Most do. The ack email can plant it automatically.
- **Ads at $30/day, disciplined:** judge video-vs-image at $150–200 spend, kill the loser, don't add budget. Ads top up the funnel; they are not the strategy. Post the ad video organically on FB/IG too — free reach from the same asset.
- **Review + referral engine ($0):** add a post-settlement workflow (engine's fixed now): day 3 review ask (Google/RateMyAgent), day 30 "know anyone thinking of selling?", anniversary touch yearly. 19 reviews → 40 by spring changes how every funnel visitor perceives you.

## PHASE 2 — September–November: harvest
- Insider monthly ("Selling in spring starts in winter" lands ~1 Sept — already drafted as Challenger C copy).
- A-list gets a personal "spring calendar is filling" call/SMS in early Sept.
- Every listing won: switch that street/suburb on — just-listed/just-sold SMS+letter to your own database first, portals second. Each sale funds nothing extra; the CRM does the fan-out.
- Off-market corner becomes real: quiet listings to the newsletter first. One off-market sale pays for a year of the entire stack.
- Retargeting only when a listing needs buyers: $5–10/day on warm audiences (site visitors + list), never cold.

## What we deliberately DON'T do (budget discipline)
- No portal upgrades, no lead-buying services, no $99/month tools — the stack replaces them.
- No ad budget increases until a full lead→appraisal→listing loop proves CPL is profitable.
- No new channels (YouTube/TikTok/letterbox at scale) until the Daily 5 habit is real. Depth before width.
