## Segment 1 — Welcome & Check-In (5 min)

Tone: warm, confident, appreciative of their patience.

- Thank them for making time
- Acknowledge it's been a long campaign — 115 days — and reassure them you've been working it hard the whole way
- Ask how they're feeling — give them space to vent if needed before you present data

---

## Segment 2 — Campaign Performance: The Numbers (7 min)

Open with the positives — the exposure has been strong. Then pivot to the conversion gap.

**What's working:**
- **82,830** total campaign exposures across realestate.com.au and beyond
- **5,351** listing views — solid reach for Redland Bay
- **171 saves** — this is a meaningful number; people genuinely like the property
- **839 eBrochures** sent to targeted buyers
- **4,698** people notified via saved searches and alerts
- Active across Facebook, Instagram, news.com.au, Yahoo and The Guardian via Audience Maximiser

**Where it's stalling:**
- Only **25 enquiries** from 5,351 views — roughly 0.5% conversion
- **0 registered inspections** despite 11 people adding it to their plan
- **0 phone/physical inspections** converting to offers (outside the current buyer)

**The key line to use:**  
> "Deb and Chris, the exposure is genuinely strong — 82,000 people have seen this property and 171 have saved it. That tells us they like it. But we're not converting that interest into inspections and offers, and after 115 days, the market is giving us a clear message we need to talk about."

---

## Segment 3 — Market Feedback & What It's Telling Us (8 min)

**The honest read — price resistance:**

The save-to-inspection ratio is the key metric here. 171 people saved the property (they love it) but nobody is registering to inspect or making offers — that's almost always a price signal, not a marketing signal.

**Competing properties their buyers are also looking at** (from the REA report):
- 3A Rosinia Court — $1.8M+ (15 days on market, already 1,900 views)
- 16 Oakland Avenue — $1.8M+ (84 days, 3,100 views)
- 107 Habitat Drive — New listing (17 days, already 3,200 views — fresh stock moving fast)
- 12 Azalea Street — $1.85M+ (59 days, 1,400 views)
- 10 Hilary Avenue — $1.5M+ (35 days)
- 3 Dennison Court — Offers Over $1.5M (45 days)

**The market context:**
- Active comparable stock sits in the $1.5M–$1.85M range
- New listings are picking up momentum fast
- At 115 days, 9 Wilohurst is now competing against fresher stock every week
- The longer it sits, the more price-sensitive buyers become ("wonder what's wrong with it")

**Actions we can still take to improve enquiry:**
- Refresh photography or hero image (small but can lift click-through)
- Consider adding a 3D tour or property walkthrough video (currently zero — competitors using these get longer engagement)
- Social media refresh — retarget saves with a new creative push
- Reach out personally to all 15 phone/SMS reveals who haven't been converted — have any of these been followed up?
- Email the 171 savers through REA with a price update if we make a move

## Segment 4 — The Buyer Situation: Asian Buyers at $1.9M (7 min)

This is the pivotal conversation. Handle it carefully — position the offer as real opportunity, not a lowball.

**The situation:**
- Interested buyers at $1.9M, subject to sale of their own property
- This is a conditional offer — the risk is their sale timeline
- $1.9M is within striking distance if vendors are at a realistic number

**Option A — Counter/Accept the $1.9M subject to sale**

> "The buyers at $1.9M are genuine. Subject to sale adds some risk around timing, but we can manage that with a short sunset clause — typically 30–60 days — so you're not sitting in limbo. If their property sells, this is done. At 115 days on market, a conditional offer from qualified buyers is something we should seriously consider."

- Ask: what's their property and how saleable is it?
- Discuss putting a time limit on the condition — e.g., 45 days with a 48-hour clause if another offer comes in
- A counter at $1.92M–$1.95M could also be worth exploring to test buyer commitment

**Option B — Price Adjustment to Create Competition**

> "The other path is a price adjustment. Right now at [current asking price], we're sitting above where comparable sales are happening. If we adjust to [price band], I believe we'll re-energise enquiry, bring those 171 savers back in, and potentially create competition — which is what gets us the best result."

- Reference: $1.85M is the top of active comparable stock right now
- A move to $1.85M–$1.9M range could trigger urgency from buyers currently on the fence
- Fresh price = REA triggers a new notification to all 4,698 people already watching the property

**The framing — don't make it a defeat:**
> "Either path is a real path to getting this sold. My job is to make sure we're making the right call with the right information. 115 days is a long time to hold on — the market has told us what it's prepared to pay, and now we decide how we want to respond to it."

**If they agree to a price adjustment:**
