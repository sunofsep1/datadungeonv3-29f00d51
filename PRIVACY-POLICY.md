## What we collect

When you request a free appraisal or contact us, we may collect:

- Your **name**;
- Your **contact details** — email address and mobile/phone number;

We only collect what we need to provide the appraisal and follow up with you.

## How we collect it

Directly from you, through the appraisal form on this site and any subsequent phone, SMS, or email contact. If you reach us via a Facebook or Instagram ad, we may also receive the details you submitted through that platform's lead form.

## Why we use it, and your consent to be contacted

We use your information to:

- Prepare and provide your **property appraisal**;
- **Contact you** about that appraisal and related property services — **by phone, SMS, and email**;
- Send you **market updates and relevant property information** where you've indicated you're happy to receive them;
- Improve our service and meet our legal and record-keeping obligations.

**By submitting the appraisal form, you consent to us contacting you by phone, SMS, and email** about your appraisal and our property services. You can withdraw this consent at any time (see "Your choices & opt-out").

## Who we share it with

We do **not** sell your personal information. We may share it with:

- **Queensland Sotheby's International Realty** and its personnel, where needed to service your enquiry;
- **Service providers** who help us operate — for example our CRM, SMS provider (Mobile Message), email provider, and hosting — who are only permitted to use it to provide those services to us;
- **Authorities or third parties** where required or authorised by law.

Our service providers may store data in Australia or overseas; where they do, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information is handled consistently with the APPs.
