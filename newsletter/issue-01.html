City Plan

Is your block suddenly worth more?

Minimum lots could drop to 300sqm, down from 400sqm, with dual occupancy at one dwelling per 350sqm — or per 300sqm on corner lots. If you’re sitting on 600–800sqm outside the canal estates, this one is worth reading properly.

The precinct cleared federal environmental assessment on 7 June. A $100 million whitewater centre, a public lagoon, and 62% of a 62-hectare site kept as conservation. My honest read on what Games infrastructure does to values — and what it doesn’t.
